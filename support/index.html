Common questions

My alarm didn't ring.

Check that alarm and notification permissions are on for Sola (Settings › Sola Alarm), the alarm is enabled, and your volume is up. On iOS 17–25, Focus can silence notifications unless time-sensitive alerts are allowed for Sola. Set a test alarm two minutes ahead before relying on a new setup.

Why can't I turn off a mission alarm from the Lock Screen?

That's the point: the alarm stops when the mission is done. If a mission can't work (no sensor or permission) or the alarm has rung for 10 minutes, Sola offers a simple math problem instead.

Why does Sola ask for Motion or Health access?

Motion powers the Shake and Steps missions. Health (read only) shows last night's sleep. All are optional.

How do I restore Pro?

Open the paywall or Settings and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the Apple ID you bought with.

How do I cancel?

Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Sola Alarm. You keep Pro until the period ends.
