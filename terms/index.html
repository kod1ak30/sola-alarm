2. What the App does

  • Schedules alarms (with Apple's AlarmKit on iOS 26 and later, and local notifications on earlier versions)
  • Wake-up missions you must complete to turn an alarm off
  • An optional gentle Early Wake Window before an alarm
  • Sleep sounds with a sleep timer
  • Wake history and charts
  • Optional Sola Pro features through in-app purchase

3. Alarm reliability

Alarms depend on things outside our control: your device settings, permissions, volume, Focus and Do Not Disturb, battery, iOS updates, and the iOS version you run. Test your alarms on your own device before relying on them. Do not rely on the App alone for critical wake-ups such as medical, travel, or safety needs.
