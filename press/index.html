About

Sola is an alarm clock for people who sleep through alarms. You choose a wake-up mission (solve a math problem, repeat a memory pattern, shake your phone, walk a number of steps, or type a phrase) and Sola keeps ringing until it's done.

What makes it different: the alarm goes quiet the moment you start working on the mission and only comes back if you stop for 30 seconds. Mornings are firm, not hostile.

  • Built on Apple's AlarmKit, so alarms ring through Silent mode and Focus
  • A mission alarm can't be dismissed from the Lock Screen; tapping Stop re-arms it
  • Never stuck: a simple math fallback after 10 minutes or if a sensor is unavailable
  • Sleep sounds with a sleep timer, wake history and on-time streaks, last night's sleep from Apple Health (read only)
  • Designed for iPhone, iPad and both displays of iPhone Duo
